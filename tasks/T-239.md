# T-239 — Guard destructive inputs — groundhog hotkey double-press, deliberate voice end-game

## Problems (audit)
- **High** — Shift+Space (default, next to Space = start timer) ran a groundhog reset with no
  confirmation and no undo; the on-screen button confirms.
- **High** — with the always-on mic, conversation like "I have to go save Zelda" toggled Zelda
  rescued (pausing the timer, writing the finish, saving a completed run) — matching was any action
  word + the target word, and since it's a *toggle*, chatter could also un-finish a run.

## Fix
- The groundhog hotkey now needs a second press within 1.5 s; the first press shows a brief
  "Press again…" toast (new non-logged `ReminderController.toast`). Catalog label "(press twice)".
- Ganon/Zelda voice toggles require a deliberate past-tense command: optional "I/we/just/finally",
  then rescued/saved/killed/defeated/beat(en), then only the target's own words (+ "the"), ≤2 words.
  Other progression toggles keep the loose rule.
