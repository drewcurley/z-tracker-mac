# T-083 — Dungeon GRAB interaction

## Description
The GRAB power tool (algorithm shipped in T-073) now has its UI, ported from the
reference `GrabHelper` + its DungeonUI interaction (`Dungeon.fs:61-160`,
`DungeonUI.fs:983-1015,1286-1412`):

- **GRAB button** in the dungeon tab bar (next to FQ/SQ), red when armed, with a
  mode instruction banner.
- **Pick up**: in grab mode, click a marked room to grab it + every contiguous
  room (and the doors between them). Hovering an unmarked-yet grab previews the
  segment (lime).
- **Drop**: click a destination; the segment moves by the offset (cut & paste,
  doors along for the ride). Hover previews the drop — lime = lands on empty,
  yellow = would overwrite. The grabbed source stays pink until dropped.
- **Keep / Undo**: after the drop a confirmation dialog lets you keep or revert
  (snapshot/restore on `DungeonRoomMap`).
- In grab mode, doors and the normal room-editing gestures (mark, pickers,
  circle, drag-paint) are suppressed; switching tabs aborts the grab.

## Acceptance criteria
