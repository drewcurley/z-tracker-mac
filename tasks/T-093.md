# T-093 — Recorder destination toggles by the widget

## Description
The two recorder-destination settings (`recorderToNewDungeons`,
`recorderToUnbeatenDungeons`) lost their UI when the recorder moved into the Info
area (T-081). Per the user, put them **back by the recorder widget** (the
reference's '...' button next to Recorder Destination): a small `ellipsis.circle`
button on the `RecorderInfoWidget` opens a popover with the two toggles.
