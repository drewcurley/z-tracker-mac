# T-015.7 — MirrorOverworld: display flip + routing

## Description
Split out of T-015.5. `MirrorOverworld` (`TrackerModelOptions.fs:97`,
default false; save field `SaveAndLoad.fs:8`) mirrors the *entire* overworld
display left-right — marks, background art, and routing all operate in the
flipped coordinate space (`Z1R_Avalonia/UI.fs:1302`,
`displayIsCurrentlyMirrored`). This is a real rendering feature, not just the
`isMirror` routing flag, which is why it's its own task rather than part of
the any-road wiring (T-015.5).

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] `mirrorOverworld` modeled as a `TrackerOptions` field (default false).
- [ ] Overworld map display flips horizontally when enabled (marks +
      background), matching the reference.
- [ ] Routing `isMirror` and `MapStateSummary.compute(mirrorOverworld:)`
      wired to it (removing `OverworldMapView.placeholderIsMirror` and the
      `mirrorOverworld: false` hardcode in `MainTrackerPlaceholderView`).
- [ ] The coast-island `[15,2]` screen-scroll routeworthy case (already in
      `MapStateSummary`) becomes reachable once mirror is live.
- [ ] Tests: coordinate-flip correctness + routing under mirror.

## Out of scope
- Recorder-warp destinations (T-018), warp-line styling.

## Risks / dependencies
- Depends on T-015.3/T-015.4 (already done). Touches the map view's
  coordinate mapping — verify mark clicks and route lines still land on the
  right (flipped) screens.
