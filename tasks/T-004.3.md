# T-004.3 — Wire "More settings" tile hiding

## Description
The startup "More settings…" popup's 12 tile-kind checkboxes
(`options.hiddenOverworldTiles`) were stored + bound but read by nothing. Wired
them into the overworld map: a marked tile whose *kind* is checked to hide now
renders **dimmed** (0.28 opacity, matching the reference's dark-X), keeping the
map uncluttered. It's revealed on **hover** (the reference's
`temporarilyDisplayHiddenOverworldTileMarks` peek) and once **Zelda is rescued**
(the endgame reveal). Ported from `OverworldMapTileCustomization.ShouldHide` +
`AsTrackerModelOptionsOverworldTilesToHide`.

- **TrackerCore:** `OverworldTileMark.hideableKind` (maps the 12 hideable marks
  to `OverworldHiddenTileKind`; nil otherwise) + `OverworldTileHiding.isKindHidden`
  (pure policy: false once Zelda rescued, else the kind's checkbox). Fully
  unit-tested.
- **UI:** `TileView` gains `kindHidden` + a `revealHovered` `@State`; the mark
  layers are wrapped in a `Group` dimmed by opacity unless hovered.
  `OverworldMapView` computes `kindHidden` per tile and gains `hasRescuedZelda`
  (threaded from `MainTrackerPlaceholderView`).

## Acceptance criteria
- [x] A marked tile of a checked kind renders dimmed; a non-hidden mark is
      bright. On-device A/B verified: an Armos tile (hidden) rendered faint, and
      the same tile switched to Money Making Game (not hidden) rendered bright.
- [x] Revealed on hover; revealed once Zelda is rescued (unit-tested).
- [x] `hideableKind` covers the 12 kinds; shops/dungeons/any-road/potion-shop
      and unknown-secret map to nil. Unit-tested.
- [x] Build clean (debug + release); full suite passes.

## Out of scope (follow-up: T-004.4)
- The two **shop** checkboxes ("Hide no-longer-relevant shop items" /
  "Always hide meat shops"): the reference filters no-longer-needed items out of
  a shop tile (composing the remaining item(s) into the two-item shop icon,
  hiding the tile only when empty). That's a shop-rendering change of its own —
  those two checkboxes don't take effect yet.
