## Description
The dedicated "OVERWORLD" and "DUNGEON / BLOCKERS / NOTES" header rows (T-123/T-124)
were pure vertical cost, pushing the timeline below the fold on a 1440p monitor. Each
section now carries a small pop-out button tucked into its **top-trailing corner**

out it shows a slim one-line placeholder. Reclaims ~2 rows + their spacing.
