# T-015 — Real GYR + destination picker + live routing state (umbrella)

## Status: split into T-015.1 … T-015.6

A grounded scoping read (2026-07-13, via a read-only investigation of
`OverworldData.fs`, `TrackerModel.fs`, `Z1R_WPF/OverworldRouteDrawing.fs`,
`Z1R_WPF/LinkRouting.fs`) established that T-015 as originally written bundles
five layers — a terrain-data port, a model-computation port, a new
data-modeling requirement, a rendering change, and a substantial new UI — and
that its premise ("mostly overworld/routing work `z-tracker-mac` already
has") was **inaccurate**: the `owInstance` terrain-capability predicates
(`OverworldData.OverworldInstance`, `OverworldData.fs:273-339`) and the shop
second-item extra-data store were **not** ported by T-006–T-010. It is
therefore split into six ordered, independently-shippable sub-tasks,
mirroring how the routing algorithm split into T-009/T-010.

recorder-warp destinations fold into **T-018** (they're derived in the
reminders/orchestration path), and `MirrorOverworld` split to **T-015.7** (a
display-flip feature, not just a routing flag).
